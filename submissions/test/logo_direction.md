
# Logo Design Direction

The proposed logo for the "Centennial Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt" should:

1. **Integrate Historical and Future Elements**: Combine elements of the京张铁路 (Jingzhang Railway) with AI symbols like neural networks or data streams.
2. **Color Scheme**: Use a blue-purple color palette to symbolize technology, intelligence, and innovation.
3. **Typography**: Modern and clean font style that conveys futurism and international appeal.
4. **Symbolism**: The logo should represent the fusion of history (京张铁路), culture (中关村), and future (AI).

This is a conceptual direction only and not an actual graphic file.
