# Formal Narrative

## Project context

The proposal addresses the 京张 corridor ("Beijing-Zhangjiakou" heritage rail
corridor) in Haidian District, positioning an AI innovation belt that connects
three key districts — 众智园AI自主创新加速区, 北京AI原点社区, and 大钟寺AI产业聚集区.
The design organizes work in three nested scopes: a coordinated research scope
of ~43.6 km² for the AI industry ecosystem, an overall design scope of
~11.4 km² around the corridor, and a key detailed design scope of ~369.3 ha
covering the three focus areas.

## Spatial structure

The spatial strategy weaves the heritage rail corridor, the 清河-小月河
blue-green corridor, and a slow-traffic network into a single framework:
university-originated innovation at the north, an open-source and talent
community at the center, and a city-scale smart economy and international
exchange cluster at the south. Land use, building footprints, roads, green and
public spaces, phasing, and constraints are expressed as structured GeoJSON
layers so that every metric in `metrics.json` can be traced back to geometry.

## Key areas

Each key area is given a distinct role and a set of spatial moves rather than a
generic "demonstration zone" label: the 众智园 area anchors the green interface
with the 清河 corridor and hosts open testing and standards governance; the
原点社区 community stitches campus, park, and neighborhood for talent
retention and startup incubation; the 大钟寺 area integrates transit-oriented
development around the 大钟寺 station with four-quadrant pedestrian
connectivity and international exchange venues.

## Implementation and AI scenarios

Implementation is staged into near-term pilots, mid-term renewal, and long-term
governance. AI scenarios — including autonomous model testing, standards
workshops, safety governance showcases, low-carbon computing experiences,
open-source communities, result publishing, talent-zone services, and
near-campus incubation — are each mapped to concrete layers, metrics, and
compliance items rather than remaining slogans.

## Data status and limitations

All boundaries and key-area polygons are provisional, inferred from public
announcement text and road names, and must be replaced with official redlines
before professional scoring. Regulatory controls, road redlines, ownership,
municipal utilities, and engineering constraints await professional
confirmation. All area values are recalculable from the submitted geometry in
EPSG:4548; any update to official geometry requires regenerating layers,
metrics, drawings, and this narrative.
